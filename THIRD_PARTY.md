# Third-party dependencies

Versions are pinned in go.mod and go.sum. Dependencies retain their own licenses:

- github.com/fsnotify/fsnotify: BSD-3-Clause
- github.com/gorilla/websocket: BSD-2-Clause
- github.com/skip2/go-qrcode: MIT
- golang.org/x/sys: BSD-3-Clause

No dependencies are vendored. Include their original copyright and license texts when distributing compiled binaries. Agent names describe compatibility and do not imply endorsement.
